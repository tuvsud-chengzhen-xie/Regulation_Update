Add a committee or subcommittee

CEN: copy the committee's XLS export link (it carries a cs= checksum). ISO: the committee catalogue URL. ASTM: the subcommittee jurisdiction page. Only the bodies' own sites are accepted. Several at once: POST /api/sources/import.

Sites that block automated access (WSTDA, BIFMA/MadCAD, ALI): open the source link in your browser, wait for the list, press Ctrl+S and choose Webpage, Complete, then use Upload page on that row. Set its transport to manual so scheduled runs stop reporting it as failed.

How matching works

Standard — follows a standard across versions, amendments and corrigenda: EN 581-1 also matches EN 581-1:2026, and F1169 matches ASTM F1169-25.

Committee — ISO/TC 29 also matches its subcommittees; F15 matches F15.42.

Keyword — matched against Standard ID, English title and committee.

A newly added committee's first capture is its whole catalogue, so it is not alerted — only later changes are.
